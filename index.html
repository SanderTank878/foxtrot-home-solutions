Raccoon Removal & Information

Professional removal and education about raccoons in your home

About Raccoons

Raccoons are highly intelligent and adaptable animals that often find their way into attics, chimneys, and crawl spaces. They are nocturnal creatures known for their dexterous front paws and distinctive black mask markings.

Lifespan

  • In the Wild: 2-3 years
  • In Captivity: 12-16 years

Common Diseases

  • Rabies
  • Raccoon Roundworm (Baylisascaris)
  • Distemper
  • Leptospirosis
  • Giardia

Signs of Raccoon Activity

  • Nocturnal noises in attic
  • Ripped soffit or vent damage
  • Droppings in attic or chimney
  • Foul odors
  • Damaged insulation
  • Pawprints in mud or dust
  • Garbage knocked over
  • Entry holes around foundation

Habitat & Diet

Raccoons are omnivores that eat fruits, vegetables, insects, small animals, and garbage. They typically nest in hollow trees, dens, attics, and crawl spaces. Peak activity occurs in late winter and spring.

Signs of Raccoon Damage

Our Raccoon Removal Process

We safely trap and remove raccoons, seal all entry points, sanitize affected areas, and remove contaminated insulation. Our humane approach ensures the safety of both your family and the animals.

Squirrel Removal & Information

Professional removal and education about squirrels in your home

About Squirrels

Squirrels are highly energetic and intelligent rodents known for their bushy tails and constant movement. They commonly invade attics and walls seeking shelter and nesting materials, causing significant damage.

Lifespan

  • In the Wild: 5-10 years
  • In Captivity: Up to 20 years

Common Diseases

  • Rabies
  • Lyme Disease
  • Typhus
  • Ringworm
  • Salmonella

Signs of Squirrel Activity

  • Constant scratching and scurrying sounds
  • Chewed wood and drywall
  • Chewed electrical wires
  • Droppings in attic
  • Torn insulation
  • Holes in soffit or roof
  • Visible squirrels entering/exiting
  • Acorn stockpiles in attic

Habitat & Diet

Squirrels are primarily active during the day and feed on nuts, seeds, acorns, and plant material. They are excellent climbers and can access upper areas of homes easily. They are most active in fall when gathering food.

Signs of Squirrel Damage

Our Squirrel Removal Process

We humanely trap and exclude squirrels, repair all entry points, and remove nesting materials. We also identify and repair damaged electrical wires and insulation to prevent future infestations.

Bat Removal & Information

Professional removal and education about bats in your home

About Bats

Bats are the only mammals capable of true flight. While they are beneficial for insect control, they can become problematic when they roost in attics and chimneys. Most bats are harmless but require professional removal.

Lifespan

  • In the Wild: 10-30 years
  • In Captivity: 30-40 years

Common Diseases

  • Rabies (transmitted through bites)
  • Histoplasmosis (fungal from droppings)
  • White-nose Syndrome

Signs of Bat Activity

  • Bat droppings (guano) in attic
  • Squeaking or chittering sounds
  • Foul odors from accumulation
  • Visible bats entering at dusk
  • Stains around entry points
  • Dead bats found in home
  • Ammonia-like smell

Habitat & Diet

Bats are primarily nocturnal and feed on insects, consuming up to 1,200 mosquitoes per night. They roost in attics, chimneys, and crevices during the day and emerge at dusk to hunt. They are most active in summer months.

Signs of Bat Activity & Damage

Our Bat Removal Process

We safely exclude bats using one-way doors and seal entry points. We professionally clean and sanitize affected areas to remove guano and prevent disease. All work complies with bat protection regulations.

Mice & Rat Control & Information

Professional removal and education about rodents in your home

About Mice & Rats

Mice and rats are prolific pests that breed rapidly and cause extensive damage. A single mouse family can become hundreds within months. They contaminate food, damage wiring, and create unsanitary conditions.

Lifespan

  • Mice: 1-2 years in the wild, 2-3 in captivity
  • Rats: 2-3 years in the wild, 3-4 in captivity

Common Diseases

  • Hantavirus
  • Leptospirosis
  • Salmonella
  • Plague
  • Lymphocytic Choriomeningitis (LCM)

Signs of Rodent Activity

  • Small droppings in cabinets and corners
  • Squeaking or scratching sounds
  • Chewed food packaging
  • Holes in walls and baseboards
  • Rub marks along walls
  • Nesting materials (shredded paper, fabric)
  • Damaged electrical wiring
  • Visible rodent sightings

Habitat & Diet

Mice and rats eat grains, seeds, and stored food items. They are excellent climbers and can access any area of your home. They are primarily nocturnal and leave trails of droppings as they move through your home constantly.

Signs of Rodent Infestation

Our Rodent Control Process

We use traps, exclusion methods, and baiting strategies to eliminate rodents. We seal all entry points, clean and sanitize affected areas, and provide prevention advice to keep rodents out permanently.
